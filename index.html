Prizes & odds

Each slice on the wheel is the same size — the weight decides where it actually lands. Weights are relative: a prize with weight 10 is ten times as likely as one with weight 1. Set a weight of 0 to show a prize without it ever landing. When a prize's stock runs out, its odds drop to 0% automatically. The spin right after a “Try again” result always lands on a real prize. Tap the icon next to the color to pick an emoji or upload an image for the slot machine reels (faded icons are suggestions).
